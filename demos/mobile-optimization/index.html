Mobile Optimization Demo

Desktop can look fine while the phone experience falls apart.

This demo uses a fake restaurant website, Ember & Grain, to show how the same business information can feel very different on a phone when the layout is not optimized.

What to look for

  • Can you read the text comfortably?
  • Are the buttons easy to tap?
  • Is the phone number easy to find?
  • Does the layout guide customers toward action?

Interactive Comparison

Same restaurant. Different phone experience.

The desktop preview stays the same. Use the phone controls to switch between an unoptimized phone layout and a mobile-friendly version.

Unoptimized phone layout

Unoptimized: The desktop layout is being forced into a phone-sized screen, making text, navigation, and buttons harder to use.

The Point

Mobile optimization is not just about shrinking a website.

A mobile-friendly site should reorganize the experience around how people actually use a phone: quick reading, easy tapping, clear contact options, and fewer obstacles between interest and action.
